Who We Are

Sobha Central is a new, mixed-use development by Sobha Realty, featuring six premium residential towers located on Sheikh Zayed Road in Dubai. It blends luxury living with commercial spaces, open parks, and retail facilities. Sobha Central offers a range of luxury apartments for sale in Dubai, designed with spacious layouts, modern interiors, and panoramic city views. The towers will offer 1- and 2-bedroom residential units, designed for luxury and comfort, with breathtaking views.

features

What make special Sobha Central

One of the standout features is a 360-metre-tall tower with 95 floors, making it the tallest residential tower in the JLT area and one of the tallest on Sheikh Zayed Road.

Prime Location

Situated on Sheikh Zayed Road, offering seamless access to Downtown, Dubai Marina, DIFC, other major city landmarks.

Built In-House

Backward integration ensures quality craftsmanship and detailed execution at every stage.

Stunning Residences

Six towers with panoramic skyline views & smart layouts, designed for optimal space, daylight, modern urban living.

Rooftop Parks

Elevated green spaces, kids’ play zones, and recreation areas like a mini golf course — crafted for all age groups.
